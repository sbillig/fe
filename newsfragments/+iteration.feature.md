Iteration is defined by `core::iter`. A `Collection` (`[T; N]`, `MemArray`, `MemSlice`, ranges, `SolArray`, `SolEnumerableSet`) yields `ref` access to each element from a cursor, `CollectionMut` yields `mut` access in mutable loops (`for x in mut xs { x += 1 }`), and `Bidirectional` adds reverse traversal. `for x in xs by d` iterates through a driver: `reversed()`, `enumerate()`, `take(n)`, `skip(n)`, `step_by(k)` and `filter(p)` traverse the base without holding it, and `map(f)` produces owned values. Drivers combine as methods (`xs.reversed().enumerate()`), and a method chain on a collection is sugar for `by`. Collections also provide `fold`, `any`, `count` and `find`, and `MemArray::collect(base, producer)` builds an array.
