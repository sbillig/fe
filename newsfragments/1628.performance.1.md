Borrow checking no longer slows down by orders of magnitude when loops or long call chains write through raw pointers held in parameters, for example structs such as `Book { items: Arr<Item>, .. }` passed by value together with a `buf: *u8` that callees write into. Possibly overwritten pointers now keep one replacement per pair of objects that may overlap instead of one per write site, offset and field. Such functions previously took minutes to check.
