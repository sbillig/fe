Owned values are destroyed exactly once when their owner gives them up: at the end of its scope, when overwritten, or for the fields a partial move leaves behind. A type releases what it holds in a `core::ops::Drop` implementation, which declares no effects. `core::mem::drop` ends a value early and `core::mem::forget` gives it up without destroying it. On EVM, a destruction that has no effect there compiles to nothing.
