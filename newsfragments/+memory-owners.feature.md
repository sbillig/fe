`MemVec`, `MemArray`, `MemBuffer` and `FixedMemBuffer` free their allocation when destroyed, and `MemVec` and `MemArray` destroy their elements; the new `core::ptr::Box<T>` holds one value in an allocation of its own. The operations that make or grow an owner have fallible forms that report failure and leave the owner as it was: `try_with_capacity`, `try_reserve`, `try_resize`, `MemVec::try_push` and `Box::try_new`. `MemVec` gains `last`, `clear` and `resize`, `MemBuffer` gains `resize`, `clear` and `try_from_span`, and slices gain `copy_from`, `copy_within`, `fill` and `zero`. `core::mem::needs_drop<T>()` tells whether destroying a `T` does anything on the target. On native targets an empty owner takes no memory from the host until it grows.
