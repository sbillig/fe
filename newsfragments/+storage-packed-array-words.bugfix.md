`StoragePackedArray<BITS>` is a map of packed words: word `w` of the array at slot `s` lies at `keccak256(w ++ s)`, the entry `w` of a `StorageMap<u256, u256>`, as in OpenZeppelin's `BitMaps` and Solady's `LibMap`. Its words used to lie consecutively from `keccak256(s)`, so a large caller-supplied index reached storage outside the array, such as other fields or map entries. `unsafe fn StoragePackedArray::at(slot)` places an array at a runtime slot, like the other collections.
