The borrow checker now checks each function against the signatures of the functions it calls, instead of inferring summaries from their bodies. A borrow a call returns comes from the callee's `self` receiver when it has one, and otherwise from every argument that holds a borrow; a `mut` argument that can hold borrows may keep any of the call's other borrows. Changing a function's body no longer changes whether its callers compile, and borrow checking is much faster. See `docs/architecture/borrow-checker.md`.
