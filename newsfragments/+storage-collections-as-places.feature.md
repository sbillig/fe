Storage collections are owners at their storage places. A `StorageMap`, `StoragePackedArray`, `StorageBytes`, `StorageHeap` or `TSlot` is no longer a `Copy` handle with a `SALT` parameter: the contract layout gives it a slot, and a map's entry for `key` lies at `keccak256(key ++ slot)` onwards, field after field, like a struct in a Solidity `mapping`. `map[key]` names the entry's place without a call (`core::ops::PlaceIndex`), so `map[key].count += 1` reads and writes one slot, and map values may be structs and other values that span several storage words. Collection methods take `self` or `mut self` and declare no effects. A type that holds a collection is storage-only (`#[storage_only]`): it cannot be constructed, copied, moved out of storage or kept in a memory value. `unsafe fn at(slot)` places a collection at a runtime slot under `RawStorage`. Storage and transient contract fields now share one slot numbering. Migration: drop `SALT` arguments and `= _` defaults, replace `map.ptr(key)`/`map.mut_ptr(key)` and `with (ptr)` blocks with `map[key]`, remove `uses (storage: Field(T))` clauses, and take collections as `mut` or view parameters instead of by value.
